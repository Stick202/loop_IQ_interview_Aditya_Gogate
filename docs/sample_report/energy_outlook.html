How to read these numbers

  • City-centre forecasts over about two weeks. They compare near-term conditions and are not a site or long-term resource assessment.
  • PV yield = GTI × 0.86 system efficiency, corrected -0.35% per °C of cell temperature above 25 °C.
  • Wind uses a generic power curve: cut-in 3.0, rated 12.0, cut-out 25.0 m/s, corrected for air density. The 200 m figure applies the same turbine at that height.
  • The API returns 100 m and 200 m wind but does not list them in its documentation. The 100 m values track the midpoint of the documented 80 m and 120 m values, so treat both heights as model estimates.
  • Confidence is a label by lead time only: high to 6 days, medium to 10, low beyond.
